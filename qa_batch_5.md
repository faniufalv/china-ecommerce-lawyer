## Q1: [Real Estate Disputes] For Expatriates: What is the statute of limitations for labor arbitration?

**A**: According to Article 27 of the Labor Dispute Mediation and Arbitration Law, the limitation period is one year from when the party knew or should have known their rights were infringed. For wage disputes during ongoing employment, the one-year limit does not apply; but after termination, claims must be filed within one year.

**法律依据**: 《社会保险法》

**领域**: 劳动法 | **语言**: English

## Q2: [Foreign Investment] For Overseas Chinese: How to apply for disability assessment after a traffic accident?

**A**: Traffic accident disability assessment process: 1) Apply to qualified judicial appraisal institution after treatment completion; 2) Submit accident report, medical records, imaging materials; 3) Institution issues assessment within 30 working days; 4) Dissatisfied parties can request re-assessment.

**法律依据**: 《民法典》侵权责任编

**领域**: 交通事故 | **语言**: English
